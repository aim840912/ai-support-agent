AI Support Agent - FAQ Knowledge Base

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We offer a 30-day return policy for all products. Items must be in their original condition and packaging. To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You can track your order by logging into your account and visiting the "Orders" section. You will also receive an email with a tracking number once your order has been shipped. Tracking updates are available within 24 hours of shipment.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available for 2-3 business days delivery. International shipping typically takes 10-15 business days depending on the destination country.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and we will send you a password reset link. The link expires after 24 hours. If you don't receive the email, please check your spam folder.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order?
A: Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, orders enter processing and cannot be modified. Please contact support immediately if you need to make changes.

Q: Do you offer customer support?
A: Yes, we offer 24/7 customer support via live chat, email, and phone. Our support team is available to help with any questions or concerns you may have. Average response time is under 2 minutes for live chat.

Q: What is your warranty policy?
A: All products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty plans are available for purchase. Warranty claims can be submitted through our support portal.
